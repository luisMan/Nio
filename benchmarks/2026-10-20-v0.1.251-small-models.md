# GPT-5 mini, GPT-5.1 and Claude Haiku 4.5 on v0.1.251 — October 20, 2026

The [October 19 benchmark](2026-10-19-v0.1.249-nio-vs-direct.md) showed three
models doing worse through Nio than they should: GPT-5 mini stalled in one run
of three, GPT-5.1 scored 76 against 93 direct, and Claude Haiku 4.5 scored 89.
We traced each low score to how Nio ended the task early, fixed those causes,
and ran the same calculator task again on builds with the fixes, three runs
per model. All of these fixes are in v0.1.251.

## Results

Scores are out of 100; 100 means every requirement was met. Nio costs come
from the gateway's usage ledger at list prices, including prompt-cache reads.

| Model | Through Nio, v0.1.251 changes | Mean | Cost per run | Through Nio, v0.1.249/250 | Direct call |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | --- | --- |
| GPT-5 mini | **100 · 100 · 94** | **98** | $0.19 | 0 · 100 · 100 (67) | 100 · 93 · 100 (98) |
| Claude Haiku 4.5 | **96 · 100 · 93** | **96** | $1.33 | 89 (one run) | 48 (one run) |
| GPT-5.1 | 69 · 79 · 87 | 78 | $0.74 | 76 (one run); 94 · 62 · 65 on v0.1.250 | 93 · 93 · 100 (95) |

- **GPT-5 mini through Nio now matches its direct calls** (98 vs 98), with no
  stalls, and every app passes its own tests. Nio still costs about twice as
  much per run.
- **Claude Haiku 4.5 averages 96 through Nio**, up from 89, and far above a
  direct call (48).
- **GPT-5.1 still does better called directly** (95 vs 78). Its zero scores
  and wrong-folder builds are gone, but it often answers with a plan instead of
  making the changes, and reruns failing tests without editing them. v0.1.251
  includes a further fix for the rerun case that was not yet in these runs.

## What changed in v0.1.251

- When a reply describes the next step but makes no tool call while requested
  work is missing, Nio asks again (up to three times) and asks for the calls
  alone instead of ending the task.
- Nio's check of the request's items now needs the exact names it lists (such
  as `M+`, `MR` or `localStorage`) and each item after a colon ("square root,
  power, sine, cosine, and tangent").
- A tool call followed by a stray quote is no longer dropped, and Nio tells the
  model exactly why a call could not be read.
- Files written into a folder named like the project itself are placed at the
  project root when the request names them there.
- Rerunning a failing check without changing anything gets the failure output
  back first, and stopping programs by name points the model to the processes
  its own task started.

## How it was run

Same calculator task and harness as October 19: the build, the enhancement,
two repair rounds and one repeat of the enhancement. These were Nio-only runs
on pre-release builds; the direct-call numbers come from the matching runs on
October 20 (GPT models) and October 19 (Haiku 4.5). Three runs per model, so a
single run still moves the average a lot.
