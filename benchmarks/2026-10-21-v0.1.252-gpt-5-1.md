# GPT-5.1 on v0.1.252 — October 21, 2026

Through v0.1.251, GPT-5.1 did worse through Nio than called directly on our
calculator benchmark (70–78 against 93–100). It answered with plans instead of
making the changes, and reran failing tests without editing them.

The cause was a setting, not the model. GPT-5.1 is the first OpenAI model that
defaults to no reasoning in the API, and Nio didn't set it, so every step of
a Nio task ran without reasoning. In v0.1.252, Nio's agent turns ask GPT-5.1
(and later GPT-5.x point releases) for `medium` reasoning, as they already do
for Claude, and give each of its replies up to 6 minutes instead of 2.

## Results

Calculator task, three runs each. The direct calls also used `medium`
reasoning and the same 6-minute limit per request, so both sides think the
same amount. Scores are out of 100; 100 means every requirement was met.

| | Run 1 | Run 2 | Run 3 | Average | Complete | Cost per run |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| **Nio v0.1.252** | **100** | **100** | 94 | **98** | 2 of 3 | $1.79 |
| Direct call | 100 | 100 | 72 | 91 | 2 of 3 | $0.64 |
| Nio v0.1.251 (no reasoning) | 65 | 76 | 69 | 70 | 0 of 3 | $0.87 |

- Every app built through Nio passes its own tests. Run 3 lacked only tests
  for decimals, negative numbers and percentages.
- In direct run 3, the enhancement had to come back as one reply, and it
  didn't finish within the 6-minute limit. Nio builds in steps, so no single
  step came near the limit.
- Reasoning is billed as output, so a GPT-5.1 task through Nio now costs about
  twice what it did, and about 2.8 times a direct call.

## How it was run

Same calculator task and harness as the [October 19
benchmark](2026-10-19-v0.1.249-nio-vs-direct.md): the build, the enhancement,
two repair rounds and one repeat of the enhancement. Three runs per side, run
side by side. Three runs are still few: one run can move the average by ten
points or more.
